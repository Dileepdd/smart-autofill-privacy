# Smart Autofill Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 18 November 2026

Smart Autofill is a browser extension that fills web forms with generated test data. It is published by Durga Dileep Dunaboyina, Visakhapatnam, India.

**In short:** Smart Autofill works entirely inside your browser. It has no servers, accounts, analytics or tracking, and nothing it reads or stores is sent to us or anyone else.

## When it runs

Smart Autofill only runs on a page after you ask it to, by clicking its toolbar icon, using its keyboard shortcut or choosing one of its right-click menu items. It does not run in the background on the sites you visit.

## What it reads on a page

When you start a fill on a page, Smart Autofill reads that page's form fields so it can decide what to enter. This includes field labels, names, placeholders and current values. It uses this information only to fill the form. The address of the page is not recorded.

## What it keeps temporarily

So that **Undo last fill** can work, the values a fill or **Clear form** replaces are held in memory on that page. This can include anything already typed into those fields, such as a password. These values are never written to storage, and they are discarded when you reload or close the page or start another fill.

## What it stores on your device

These items are kept in your browser's local extension storage:

- Your settings, such as country, email domain and date format
- Any values you choose to enter, such as names, a company, a test password pattern and custom rules
- Your saved test profiles
- Usage statistics shown on the options page: how many forms and fields were filled, counts by field type and test data mode, an estimate of time saved, and daily totals for the last 90 days. These are numbers only, with no field values and no page addresses. You can reset them at any time.

They stay on your device and are not synced to any account. Avoid entering real personal information or real passwords in these settings, because they are not encrypted by the extension.

## Generated data

Names, addresses, phone numbers, identifiers and similar values are generated at random on your device. They are meant for testing and do not describe real people or real documents, although a generated value could match a real one by chance.

## What it does not do

Smart Autofill does not:

- Send data over the network
- Sell or share data with anyone
- Use data for advertising or to determine creditworthiness
- Record your browsing history
- Fill payment card, one-time code or CAPTCHA fields, unless you create a custom rule or pick a value for one yourself
- Submit forms

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `activeTab` | To access only the tab where you clicked the icon or a menu item, and only at that moment |
| `scripting` | To run the filling code in that tab |
| `storage` | To save your settings and test profiles on your device |
| `contextMenus` | To show options when you right-click the toolbar icon or a text field |

## Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes its settings and test profiles from your browser.

## Limited Use

For the version distributed through the Chrome Web Store, the use of information received by Smart Autofill complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If the way data is handled changes, the extension will tell you before the change applies.
